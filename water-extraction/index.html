How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
