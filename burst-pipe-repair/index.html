Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. One broken line can put out 4 to 8 gallons every minute, so 240 to 480 gallons an hour may be running through floors, ceilings and walls. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Torrance Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Torrance and the nearby communities. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
